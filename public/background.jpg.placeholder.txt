Place your background image at:

  /public/background.jpg

(rename or copy your image to that exact filename)

The app already references /background.jpg as the global background.
Once that file exists, the glass UI will sit on top of it across every page.

For best results with the glass system:
- Use an image that is at least 1920x1080px
- Prefer a darker or more muted image if you want text to stay readable
- Avoid pure white or extremely bright photos

You can also change the image path in src/app/layout.tsx (GLOBAL_BG_IMAGE_URL)
and in src/app/page.tsx (hero section) to match a different filename.
